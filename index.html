About the Candidate

Pembroke Born.
Pembroke Built.

I've called Pembroke home my entire life — from growing up and attending Equinox and Fellows High School, to building my career and raising my family right here in this city.

Thirty years ago, my dad opened the doors to a multidisciplinary health centre in Pembroke, and I've had the privilege of growing alongside that business — from working the floor at Nutrition House, to managing our retail store, to becoming CEO of the corporation two years ago. That journey taught me what it means to show up, work hard, and serve this community with integrity.

But my commitment to Pembroke doesn't stop at work. I've served as Vice-President of the Chamber of Commerce, Chairman of the Board at Phoenix Centre, and currently sit on the Marianhill Board and the OPP Detachment Board for the City of Pembroke. I know this community — its people, its challenges, and its incredible potential.

I decided to run for City Council because I got tired of watching my tax dollars go nowhere. After reading 13 Ways to Kill a Community by Doug Griffiths, something clicked. We need to stop thinking short-term and start asking: what do we want Pembroke to look like in 10 or 20 years? We need a city where our seniors can age at home and stay active, where our kids have things to do and places to go, and where prevention and education are the cornerstones of every decision we make.

I'm kind, compassionate, and driven. I listen. I operate with integrity. And I'm ready to bring real leadership to City Council — for you, for your family, and for the Pembroke we all deserve.

My Platform

What I'll Fight For

Pembroke deserves leadership that focuses on real issues affecting real families. Here are the three priorities I'll champion on City Council.

🛡️

Community Safety

Every Neighbourhood, Every Street

Feeling safe at home and in our community isn't a luxury — it's a baseline. Pembroke families deserve well-resourced emergency services and streets where everyone feels secure.

  • Advocate for adequate staffing for fire, police & paramedic services
  • Improve pedestrian safety, street lighting & traffic calming
  • Support community programs that address root causes of crime
  • Work with residents to identify safety priorities neighbourhood by neighbourhood
💰

Fiscal Responsibility

Smart Spending. Accountable Government.

Every tax dollar belongs to Pembroke residents. I'll ensure City Council spends wisely, prioritizes essential services, and keeps our finances transparent and sustainable.

  • Push for a clear, publicly-accessible budget process
  • Scrutinize spending to protect core services over unnecessary costs
  • Champion value-for-money in all infrastructure and capital projects
  • Keep tax increases modest and justified to Pembroke families
🏠

Affordable Housing

A Home in Pembroke for Everyone

Young families, seniors, and working residents are being priced out. Pembroke needs a council that takes housing affordability seriously and acts with urgency.

  • Support zoning reforms that allow diverse, attainable housing types
  • Encourage partnerships with non-profits and developers on affordable builds
  • Advocate for seniors' housing options that keep our elders in Pembroke
  • Streamline the approval process to get housing built faster
